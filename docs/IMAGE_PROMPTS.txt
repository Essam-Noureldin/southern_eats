================================================================
Sam's Southern Eatery — image prompts for menu items
================================================================

Use these prompts in Midjourney, DALL-E, Adobe Firefly, Imagen, etc.
to generate the missing dish photography. Once each image is ready:

  1. Save as public/images/dish-<slug>.jpeg matching the slug below
  2. Compress to ~600KB at quality 80 via squoosh.app
  3. Open lib/menu.ts, find the matching entry, add
     imageUrl: "/images/dish-<slug>.jpeg"
  4. Hard-refresh /menu — typographic placeholder swaps to the photo,
     view-transition morph starts working too


----------------------------------------------------------------
HOUSE STYLE PREAMBLE — prepend to every prompt below
----------------------------------------------------------------

Editorial food photography for Sam's Southern Eatery. Warm natural
window light, weathered honey-colored wood or cream linen surface,
slight overhead angle (~30°), hand-breaded golden fry color, generous
Southern portions, no people, no text overlay, 1:1 square crop, true-
to-life food styling. Side ramekin of comeback sauce, ranch, or
remoulade where it makes sense.
