SAM'S SOUTHERN EATERY — DISH IMAGE PROMPTS
============================================

Each prompt is written for a square-format image generator (1024x1024
or larger). The dish should fill ~70-80% of the frame so it crops well
to the existing DishCard aspect-square layout.

Filename target for each prompt is shown after the dish name. Save the
generated image to /public/images/<filename> and update IMG.<key> in
lib/menu.ts to point at it.

GLOBAL BRAND DIRECTION (apply to every prompt unless overridden):
- Warm Southern afternoon light, golden-amber tones, soft window light
  from upper left
- Rustic surfaces: weathered dark wood, cream parchment, cast iron,
  enamel diner plates, red-and-white checkered paper
- Editorial food photography, shallow depth of field, ~45° angle
  unless otherwise noted
- Tight crop, dish dominant, props secondary
- No people, no text overlays, no logos, no watermarks
- Color palette to avoid: cool blues, neon, purple
- Color palette to embrace: cream, deep red, butter yellow, moss green,
  warm browns and ambers

====================
NOTES FOR ESSAM
====================

- The most important hero image is JUMBO SHRIMP (#5) — it's both the
  homepage hero behind the H1 and the carousel signature dish. Spend
  the most time iterating this one.
- For the FAMILY portions (#18-20) the flat-lay angle is deliberate
  — those should look like a feast, not a single plate.
- KIDS dishes (#25-26) should be visually lighter than the adult
  dishes — bright cream/wood backdrop instead of dark wood — so the
  category reads as different at a glance.
- DRINKS (#27-28) should photograph from slightly lower than the
  food shots so the height of the mason jar reads.
- After generating, optimise each image to ~600KB via squoosh.app
  before placing in /public/images.
- Update lib/menu.ts: today most dishes share images via the IMG
  helper. Once individual images exist, replace each MenuItem's
  imageUrl with the dedicated path (e.g. "/images/dish-coleslaw.jpg").
